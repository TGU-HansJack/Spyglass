Spyglass Fork 0.6.3
Vintage Story 1.22.3-1.22.7

Based on Spyglass 0.6.1 by Fuami.
Original project: https://github.com/fuami/Spyglass
Original release page: https://mods.vintagestory.at/spyglass
Fork author/maintainer: HansJack

Fork changes: third-person effect gating, synchronized vignette/HUD handling,
optional Scribe HUD compatibility, player lifecycle guards and state cleanup,
and independent spyglassfork identifiers, assets and configuration.

Rain simulation and height-field rendering adapted from
Anti-Productivity RainyGlass (APRG), RainSim.cs and Shaders.hlsl.
The Vintage Story integration uses the game scene texture and OpenGL;
it does not include APRG desktop capture, window overlays or Direct3D code.
