Fork changes: third-person effect gating, synchronized vignette/HUD handling,
optional Scribe HUD compatibility, player lifecycle guards and state cleanup,
and independent spyglassfork identifiers, assets and configuration.

Frost is generated as seeded optical prisms by the mod and its shader. No frost
photograph, image texture, or decoded reference-image data is included in this
release. The user-supplied frost image was used only as a visual reference for
the feathered branching arrangement.
